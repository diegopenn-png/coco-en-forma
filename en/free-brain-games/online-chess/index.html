Playing tip

Before moving a defender, check which piece it currently protects. Picture your opponent’s best reply to the move you have in mind.

Try a small challenge

Your king is in check from a knight. Can you escape by putting another piece between them?

Show the explanation

No. A knight jumps, so its check cannot be blocked. You need a legal king move or a legal capture of the attacking knight.
