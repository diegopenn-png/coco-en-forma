Battleships

Playing tip

After two hits in a line, try the squares at either end. Ships can touch in this version, so a square next to a sunken ship can still hold another one.

Try a small challenge

You hit D4 and D5, and the ship is still afloat. Which untried squares extend that line?

Show the explanation

D3 and D6. Fire at one end first. If those hits belong to the same ship, it must run along that row.
