Argentine Truco card game

Playing tip

Check for two cards of the same suit before calling envido. A six and a seven of the same suit are worth 33 together: 20 plus their two values.

Try a small challenge

You hold the six and seven of coins and a king of cups. How many points can you claim for envido?

Show the explanation

33. The six and seven share a suit, so you add 20 to their values: 20 + 6 + 7.
