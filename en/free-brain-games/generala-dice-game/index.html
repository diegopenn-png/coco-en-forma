Playing tip

With three fours, check whether your fours category or four of a kind is still open. That can change which dice are worth rolling again.

Try a small challenge

On your first roll you get 4, 4, 4, 2 and 5. Your fours category is open. What could you score immediately?

Show the explanation

12 in fours. You can also keep the three fours and roll the other two dice again to try for a bigger combination.
