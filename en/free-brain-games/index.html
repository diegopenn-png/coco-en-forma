Choose a challenge that interests you

In Memory, you find matching pairs by remembering where each picture appeared. Connect Colours asks you to join dots while leaving room for other paths. Sudoku is about ruling out numbers using rows, columns and boxes. These are different ways to play: follow your curiosity, without having to choose the hardest puzzle first.

For turn-based strategy, try Chess. Generala combines dice with decisions about which results to keep, while Battleship challenges you to locate ships on a grid. Each game has its own rules. Reading them first helps you understand the aim, the scoring and how a round ends.

Make a little room for play

Play on your own or share a device for a local game. When playing as a family, talk about a choice after the round: what you noticed, what you changed and what you might try next time. A result describes that particular game and can be a useful starting point for a conversation.

You can also change the language from the menu after signing in. Coco remembers your choice as you move between pages. Installing the web app adds a shortcut to your home screen. To try a different activity, return to the menu and open its guide.

Frequently asked questions

Are these games free?

Yes. All Coco games and accounts are free. You don’t need a payment card or subscription to play, and there are no ads. Eterna offers optional school support through a subscription. Coco Eneagrama is a paid self-awareness assessment with a PDF report. Both services are separate from the games.

When do I need an account?

Memory, Sudoku and Connect Colours require a free Coco account. Online rooms and profile features also require you to sign in. Local Chess, Generala and Battleship games can be started without registering.

Do I have to download an app?

You can play directly in your browser. If you would like a shortcut on your home screen, you can also install Coco as a web app.

Is all the content available in 15 languages?

The interface offers 15 languages. Some word and knowledge games keep their content in Spanish and display a notice. Check the game guide before starting.

Can we play as a family?

Yes. Take turns, share a local game or use an available online mode. A parent or guardian must create or authorise an account for a child.
